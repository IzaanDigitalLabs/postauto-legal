Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

PostAuto ("we", "our", "the app") is a social media automation tool that publishes content to Facebook Pages on behalf of authorized page administrators. This policy explains what data we access and how we use it.

Data We Access

  • Facebook Page Access Tokens – used solely to publish posts to your Facebook Page on your behalf.
  • Facebook Page ID – used to identify which page to post to.
  • No personal Facebook profile data is accessed or stored.

How We Use Your Data

Access tokens and page identifiers are stored encrypted (AWS KMS) and used exclusively for automated posting to your authorized Facebook Page. We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party.

Data Retention

Credentials are retained as long as you use the service. You may request deletion at any time (see Data Deletion below). Published post records are retained for analytics purposes and deleted on request.

Third-Party Services

  • Meta (Facebook) – content is published via the Meta Graph API.
  • AWS – infrastructure hosting, encrypted credential storage.
  • NewsAPI – used to source article headlines for content generation.
