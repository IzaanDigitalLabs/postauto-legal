Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 14, 2026

If you would like to delete your data from PostAuto, follow the steps below. All stored credentials, page tokens, and associated posting records will be permanently removed.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to jahidul.activity@gmail.com with the subject line: "PostAuto Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include the Facebook Page name or Page ID associated with your account.
  3. We will confirm deletion within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your encrypted Facebook Page Access Token
  • Your Facebook Page ID and associated account record
  • All content job records associated with your account

Revoking App Access Directly

You can also immediately revoke PostAuto's access to your Facebook Page at any time:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings → Business Integrations
  2. Find PostAuto and click Remove

This revokes the access token immediately. Your data will be deleted from our systems within 7 days.
