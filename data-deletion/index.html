Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: June 10, 2026

If you would like to delete your data from PostAuto, follow the steps below. All stored credentials, platform tokens, and associated posting records will be permanently removed.

How to Request Full Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to jahidul.activity@gmail.com with the subject line: "PostAuto Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address associated with your PostAuto account.
  3. We will confirm deletion within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your encrypted Facebook Page access token and Page ID
  • Your Instagram Business Account ID (if connected)
  • Your encrypted LinkedIn access token and Person ID
  • Your PostAuto account record and profile data
  • All generated content job records associated with your account
  • All generated images stored in our S3 bucket for your account

Revoking Platform Access Directly

You can also immediately revoke PostAuto's access on each platform without contacting us. This invalidates the access token in our system right away.

Facebook & Instagram

  1. Go to Facebook Settings → Security and Login → Business Integrations
  2. Find PostAuto and click Remove

LinkedIn

  1. Go to LinkedIn Settings → Data Privacy → Other Applications
  2. Find PostAuto and click Remove

After revoking access directly on a platform, your credential data will be removed from our systems within 7 days. To delete all remaining PostAuto data, please still send us the email request above.
