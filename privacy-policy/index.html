Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

PostAuto ("we", "our", "the app") is a social media automation tool that publishes AI-generated content to your connected social media accounts on your behalf. This policy explains what data we access, how we store it, and your rights regarding that data.

We connect to the following platforms: Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn. This policy covers all three.

1. Data We Access per Platform

Facebook

DataPurpose
Facebook Page Access TokenPublish posts to your Facebook Page on your behalf
Facebook Page ID & nameIdentify which page to post to; display in the PostAuto UI
Instagram Business Account ID (if linked to your Page)Publish posts to your Instagram Business account

We do not access your personal Facebook profile, friends list, messages, or any data beyond what is listed above.

Instagram

DataPurpose
Instagram Business Account IDIdentify the Instagram account to post to
Instagram page name & handleDisplay in the PostAuto UI; include in generated post images

Instagram access is granted via the same Facebook OAuth flow. We use the Instagram Content Publishing API only. No follower data, DMs, or analytics are accessed.

LinkedIn

DataPurpose
LinkedIn OAuth Access TokenPublish posts to your personal LinkedIn profile on your behalf
LinkedIn Person ID (sub)Construct the author URN required by the LinkedIn API
LinkedIn display nameDisplay in the PostAuto UI to confirm which account is connected

We use the openid profile w_member_social OAuth scopes. We do not access your LinkedIn connections, messages, job history, or any other profile data beyond your name and account identifier.

2. How We Store Your Data

  • Access tokens for all platforms are encrypted at rest using AWS Key Management Service (KMS) with per-account encryption contexts. Tokens are never stored in plaintext.
  • Account identifiers and display names (Page IDs, Person IDs, handles) are stored in Amazon DynamoDB associated with your PostAuto account.
  • Generated post content (text, images) is stored in Amazon S3 and DynamoDB for approval workflow and historical reference.
  • All data is stored in the US East (N. Virginia) AWS region.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Platform credentials are used exclusively to publish content that you have approved (or set to auto-publish) to your connected accounts.
  • Display names and identifiers are used within the PostAuto UI to show you which accounts are connected.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.
  • We do not use your data to train machine learning models.

4. Third-Party Services

PostAuto integrates with the following third-party services to deliver its functionality:

  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram) – Graph API for publishing. Subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • LinkedIn – UGC Posts API for publishing. Subject to LinkedIn's Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) – Cloud infrastructure (Lambda, DynamoDB, S3, KMS, SES, Bedrock). Subject to AWS's Privacy Notice.
  • Amazon Bedrock / Anthropic Claude – AI model used to generate post content. Prompts and outputs are not used to train third-party models under our enterprise agreement.

5. Data Retention

  • Platform credentials are retained for as long as your PostAuto account is active.
  • Generated post records are retained for historical reference and deleted on request.
  • You may request full deletion of your data at any time (see Data Deletion Instructions).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Revoke platform access at any time directly via each platform's settings (Facebook Business Integrations, LinkedIn App Settings) — this immediately invalidates the access token on our end.
  • Disconnect individual platforms within PostAuto without deleting your account.

7. Children's Privacy

PostAuto is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as we add new platform integrations or change our data practices. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of PostAuto after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
